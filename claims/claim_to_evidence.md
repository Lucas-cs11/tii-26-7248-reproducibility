## Evidence authority

* The independent audit (`experiments/01_scalability/results/audit.json`)
  recomputes every hash and pre-registered field from the raw records.
* `results/statistics.json` is produced by the analysis pipeline from the raw
  records and cross-checked against the audit.
* Every result file is generated by the scripts shipped here and covered by
  `MANIFEST.sha256`.

## Machine-readable identifiers

`results/*.json` are generated evidence and retain the identifiers of the
pre-registered protocol; the documents describe the experiments descriptively.
Both use the same labels, so the mapping is unambiguous.
