# Illustrative Time Petri Net case

The fixed net and the recorded analysis/adjustment results for the illustrative
Time Petri Net used to introduce the diagnosis and firing-interval-adjustment
workflow.

Contents: `model/` (places, transitions, arcs, initial marking and the Graphviz
view), `results/` (analysis and adjustment outputs).

The net can be analysed with the verifier shipped in
`../../01_scalability/code/`; this folder keeps the case definition and the
recorded outputs.
