# Illustrative Time Petri Net case

The fixed net, the recorded analysis/adjustment results and the figures for the
illustrative Time Petri Net used to introduce the diagnosis and
firing-interval-adjustment workflow.

Contents: `model/` (fixed net: places, transitions, arcs, initial marking and
the Graphviz view), `results/` (analysis and adjustment outputs),
`figures/` (rendered versions of the case figure), `MANIFEST.sha256`.

The net can be analysed with the verifier shipped in
`../../01_scalability/code/`; this folder keeps the case definition and the
recorded outputs, not a second copy of the implementation.
